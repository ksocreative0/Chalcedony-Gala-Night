15 Years of
God's Mercy

The Staff Award and Gala Night is a grand celebration marking 15 years of excellence, growth, impact and outstanding achievements. It is an opportunity to reflect on our journey, celebrate our successes and recognise both past and present staff members whose dedication, loyalty, hard work and years of service have contributed immensely to the growth and development of our institution. The evening will honour their invaluable contributions and celebrate the people behind our success.
A major highlight of the evening will be the official launch of our School Alumni Association creating a platform for former students to reconnect, strengthen bonds and contribute to the continued growth of their alma mater. As we celebrate this remarkable milestone, we look forward to an evening of glamour, entertainment, awards and heartfelt appreciation as we honour our legacy, celebrate our people and embrace the future.
15 Years of Excellence. A Legacy of Impact. A Future of Possibilities!

Executive Director
Mrs. Gbemisola Olowookere
